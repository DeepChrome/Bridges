# Grammar sequence

The order in which Bridges introduces grammar, and why. Decided 2026-09-06 from
what the established courses agree on; the owner's brief was "less complicated to
more complicated" and a spine a learner can leave for side quests and come back to.

## What the courses do

| Source | Order of the major points |
|---|---|
| Cornell *Beginning Russian Grammar*, grammar reviews I–XIV | nouns/pronouns → **accusative, gender, adjectives, present verbs** → **prepositional**, conjugation → nominative plural, **genitive**, animacy, aspect intro → **dative**, prepositions → **instrumental** → case endings → aspect pairs → reflexives, imperative, time → numerals → motion verbs, conditionals → comparatives, prefixes → participles |
| Duolingo Russian (95 units, 2025 data) | intro: introductions, basic sentences, **plurals**, **where you are** (prepositional), trips → A1: **genitive**, possession, numbers, likes, **past**, family, weather, months, clothes → A1 cont.: **dative**, **instrumental**, genitive plural, reflexives, commands, relative clauses, future → A2: prefixes, participles, abstract topics |
| RussianForFree basic course (14 lessons) | first words → **present tense** singular/plural → conjugations → **past** → reflexives → possessives & adjectives → cases intro → **aspect** → imperative → future → motion verbs |
| TORFL A1 (ТЭУ) requirements | alphabet; **nominative, prepositional, accusative**; genitive and dative introduced; gender agreement; **present and simple past**; basic aspect pairs (брать/взять, говорить/сказать); basic motion verbs; numbers and time |
| Red Kalinka / howtogetfluent A2 description | all six cases met by the end of A2, prepositional and accusative first, common prepositions; numbers with cases; time expressions |

Consensus, in order: no articles / no "is" → present-tense verbs (two conjugations)
→ gender and agreement → plural → prepositional (location) → accusative (object,
direction) → past tense → genitive (possession, absence, quantity) → dative (likes,
age) → instrumental (with, by, as) → aspect → future → reflexives → imperative →
motion verbs → numerals → comparatives → conditionals → participles.

## What Bridges does

The spine has eight units, one grammar card each; the branches that follow a spine
unit carry one card each and may only use what the spine has introduced by then.

| Chapter | Spine card | Branch cards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No "a", no "the", no "is" | family: possessives agree · time: numbers change the noun |
| 2 | Two verb patterns (present tense) | food: есть/пить, two irregulars · school: учить/учиться/изучать |
| 3 | Every noun has a gender | home: the ending tells the gender · clothes: adjectives agree |
| 4 | Making things plural | city: places in the plural · travel: идти/ходить |
| 5 | Saying where something is (prepositional) | body: what hurts is the subject · nature: в лесу́, на мо́ре |
| 6 | The accusative marks the object | work: having and not having (first genitive) · animals: animate accusative |
| 7 | The past tense agrees with the subject | emotion: liking is done to you (dative) · speech: по-русски |
| 8 | Two aspects of every verb | military: with + instrumental · tech: by + dative · sport · art · politics: genitive plural |

Not yet covered by a card, in the order they should arrive: the future tense,
reflexive verbs, the imperative, comparatives, conditionals. They belong to the
side-quest branches planned in ROADMAP A24, not to the spine.
