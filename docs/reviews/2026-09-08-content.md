# Bridges — content audit (what the learner sees), 2026-09-08

Scope: `native/assets/data.json` (built 07 Sep 2026), `native/src/images.js`, the four SQLite
databases (read-only), `data/audio.json`, `data/raw/images/manifest.json` (v2 entries, harvest still
running). Scripts in the scratchpad: `a1_units.py … a12_yo.mjs`, `stt_check.ps1`. Nothing in the
repository was modified.

## Verdict

1. **Not yet.** The corpus, the translations and the audio are sound; the *joins* around them are not.
   Roughly one spine word in nine is a stub or a wrong word, and the commonest words in the language
   resolve to the wrong dictionary entry when tapped.
2. **Risk 1 — the frequency count is wrong at the root.** `build_topics.py` and `build_site.py` count a
   token once per *paradigm slot* that shares its key, so «лёт» (flight), «быль» (true story), «деть»,
   «тут» (mulberry), «вод», «лета» (Lethe) sit on chapters 2–8's spine while «если», «или», «два»,
   «надо», «нужно», «пожалуйста», «никогда», «вчера» are taught nowhere.
3. **Risk 2 — shared forms resolve to the wrong lemma everywhere** (`IX[key][0]`, search ranking,
   cloze options, Hear/Say grading, video chips): «нет» → житься, «уже» → узкий, «после» → посол,
   «при» → переть, «два» → двух, «три» → тереть, «потом» → пот, «тут» → mulberry. 18 % of corpus tokens
   sit under a shared key; 1,757 of the 1,912 speech-pool sentences contain one.
4. **Risk 3 — 25 unit words (every ё headword, plus Россия/Москва) open with no examples and no
   paradigm** although the payload carries both: `core/entry.js:39` folds the key, `native/src/data.js:106`
   does not. Quiz prompts are ambiguous for 202 unit words (two words, one first sense).
5. What is trustworthy: translations (40/40 sampled faithful), paradigm tables where they appear (30/30
   sampled correct, `describeForm` right on 3,769/4,139 example forms), video moments (18,054/18,054
   structurally sound), audio provenance (190 Tatoeba files refetched after the id fix; no English found).

## Findings, ranked by harm

### 1. The curriculum's frequency count multiplies by paradigm slots — the spine is built on it
- **What.** `forms` has several rows per (key, lemma) — «лёт» owns key «лет» three times (sg_nom, sg_acc,
  pl_gen), «быль» owns «были» five times. `build_topics.py:393-407` builds `owners` as a *list*, so
  `counts[lid] += n` runs once per row: «быль» ×5, «лета» ×53, «лето» ×13.6, «евро» ×13, «тот» ×10,
  «деть» ×6.9 (`a7_topics_count.py`). `build_site.py:397-410` (`top`, `corpus_n`) has the same join, so
  the lemma order in `L` — which is also the index priority — is inflated the same way: 134 of the top
  300 lemmas carry `n` ≥ 2× their real token count.
- **Evidence.** Re-running the same algorithm with a set instead of a list: **58 words leave the
  224-word spine** (among them деть, тут-noun, лета, вод, сей, густой, евро, помочь-noun, and real words
  like молодой, мать, дочь) and **58 join** — надо, нужно, играть, никогда, пойти, купить, дать, ждать,
  если, или, два, вчера, пожалуйста, тогда, вот, даже, без, под, над, часто, более, стол, пить, писать,
  бояться, забыть, помочь-verb, рассказать. Today none of если / или / два / надо / нужно / пожалуйста /
  никогда / вчера / завтра / тогда is in any unit (they are closed-class, so no branch can take them).
- **Why it bites the learner.** Chapter 2 teaches «лёт: flight» with four examples that all mean "years"
  («Рита замужем уже пять лет»); chapter 3 «быль: fact, true story» with «Вы были в Италии?»; chapter 5
  «деть: put, do» with «Я звоню маме каждый день»; chapter 6 «лета: Lethe, years, age»; chapter 8 «вод:
  waters, medicinal waters» with «Выпей воды». Placement and section tests draw from the same list.
- **Fix (pipeline).** `owners.setdefault(key, set()).add(lid)` in `build_topics.py`, and `count(distinct
  tk.item_id, tk.idx)` (or a `(key, lemma_id)`-deduped `forms` view) in `build_site.py` `top`/`corpus_n`.
  Then re-read the spine names (`CHAPTERS`) because the bands shift. Learner state keys on the word, so
  history survives the reshuffle; `unit[...]` lesson slots will not (lesson indices move).
- **Effort.** 1 hour for the count; half a day to re-read the eight spine lists and re-run the simulator.

### 2. The wrong homograph is chosen as the headword, and the learner is taught it
- **What.** Two rules in `build_topics.py` pick the row to teach: the heads rule (`fold(bare) == key`
  credits a shared key to the one lemma whose headword it is — but `fold` turns ё→е, so «лёт» *is* the
  headword «лет» and takes all 100 tokens of "years" exclusively, `build_topics.py:403-405`); and the
  dedup `POS_RANK = {"other": 9}` (`:425-445`) which prefers a noun/verb row over the adverb/particle
  row of the same spelling — so «тут» the mulberry beats «тут» "here", «есть» "eat" beats «есть» "there
  is", «помочь» the archaic noun (3 slots ⇒ ×3) beats the verb.
- **Evidence (`a3_tokens.py`).** тут-noun [id 11] "mulberry fruit; mulberry tree": 117 tokens, every one
  «Тут никого нет»-type; тут-other [58793] "here; now" not taught. есть-verb "eat": 145 of 195 tokens are
  «у меня есть». мыть "wash": 91 of 101 tokens are «мой/мою/моём» (my). сей "This": 54 «сих» (до сих
  пор) + 42 «сей». больший "greater": all tokens are forms of большой. `resolveWord("тут")` and
  `search("тут")` both return the mulberry; the video library lists 149 «тут» moments under that entry.
- **Spine words that should not be headwords (26 of 224, 12 %).** Case stubs glossed "genitive of I":
  меня, мне, его, её, нас, вам, ему, вас, тебя, нам, чём (11). Wrong word: лёт, лета, быль, деть, тут,
  вод, помочь-noun, мыть, больший, сей (10). Doubtful: все + всё (same row twice, same examples), есть
  "eat" (examples are "have"), евро (rank 3661), густой (rank 2815), должный "must".
- **Fix (pipeline).** (a) `build_transcripts.resolve()` already has the right rule — closed-class headword
  beats the inflection it could be, stubs demoted, independent frequency with a margin; lift it into
  `panel.py` and use it in `build_topics.py` for the pool count and in `build_site.py` for the order of
  `index[key]`. (b) A curated `data/curated/lemma_overrides.json` (key → lemma id) for the dozen the rule
  cannot settle (лет→год, тут→"here", есть→"there is", мой→мой). (c) Extend `STUBS` in `build_topics.py`
  with the 11 case-form rows (or drop any lemma whose gloss matches `STUB_GLOSS`).
- **Effort.** One day including re-reading the spine.

### 3. `IX[key][0]` — the app's first choice for a shared form — is wrong for the commonest words
- **What.** `build_site.py:510` appends lemma indices to `index[key]` in `order` (the inflated frequency
  order), and every consumer takes `[0]`: word links (`resolveWord`), `search`, cloze option labels,
  `sentenceLemmas` (Hear/Say/Scene grading and the "which word did you hear" options), `heardIn`.
- **Evidence (`a8_ix.py`, `a11_search.mjs`).** 687 corpus keys are shared, carrying 16,782 tokens (18 %).
  In the top 120 shared keys, 13 resolve to a plainly wrong lemma: нет→житься (125 tokens), уже→узкий
  (164), при→переть (103), после→посол (95), надо→над (82), два→двух (54), три→тереть (34), потом→пот
  (34), почти→почтить (36), домой→домыть (28), тут→mulberry (39), дело→деть (16), помочь→noun (38);
  ~20 more send an adverb to its adjective (просто→простой, хорошо→хороший, быстро→быстрый, часто→
  частый, нужно→нужный — `describeForm` then says "Short form · n", defensible but not what a learner
  tapping «хорошо» wants). Dictionary search shows the same ranking: typing «нет» lists "житься: to be
  able to live" first; «years» lists "лета: Lethe" first. Consequence for grading: a Hear step containing
  «нет» grades the card of «житься»; the scheduler then schedules a word the learner never met.
- **Fix (pipeline).** Order `index[key]` with the resolver from finding 2 (headword of a closed-class
  lemma first, real-POS headword next, stubs last, then independent frequency); write the winner first.
  `core/search.js` needs no change if the order is right. Add a build check that prints the first choice
  for the 100 commonest shared keys so a regression is visible.
- **Effort.** Half a day; the test list is in `a8_ix.py`.

### 4. Every ё headword — and Россия, Москва — shows no examples and no paradigm in the app
- **What.** `core/entry.js:39` looks the studied lemma's dictionary twin up as
  `deepIndex().get(fold(entry.b))`; `native/src/data.js:106` builds that map on the *unfolded* `d.b`.
  `fold` maps ё→е and lower-cases, so «ребёнок» asks for «ребенок» and «Россия» for «россия» — miss,
  `t = []`, `x = []`.
- **Evidence (`a12_yo.mjs`, run through the identical map construction).** 24 unit words contain ё:
  0 have tables, 1 has examples (всё, only because a separate «все» row exists). Across the 4,006
  curriculum lemmas: 86 with ё, 84 without examples, 85 without tables. 25 unit words open with no
  example at all: лёт, ребёнок, Россия, её, ещё, самолёт, учёный, чём, Москва, серьёзный, молодёжь,
  тётка, тёща, тётя, мёд, ковёр, дешёвый, шёлковый, тёплый, лёд, козёл, актёр, чёрт, ружьё,
  радиоприёмник — the payload carries 4 examples for each (`unit_words.tsv`), so this is the join, not
  the data. Cloze questions cannot be built for them either (`clozeFor` reads `w.x`).
- **Second half of the same join.** The map keeps the *first* deep row per bare form, and OpenRussian
  lists homographs as separate rows sorted by id: мочь → noun "power, might", знать → noun "nobility",
  русский → noun "Russian man", больной → noun "patient", правый, простой, рабочий, полицейский, святой
  likewise. The adjective/verb entry then gets a noun's slots, `buildTables` finds no `decl_`/`presfut_`
  slots, and the table is empty: 13 unit adjectives and 3 unit verbs (мочь, знать, стать) have no
  paradigm in the app.
- **Fix.** Key the map on `fold(d.b)` *and* POS (`fold(b) + "|" + p`), fall back to fold alone; or ship
  the twin's row index on the studied lemma from `build_site.py` (it knows both) and drop the lookup.
  Add a jest/core test: "every unit word with a `refs` field hydrates with `x.length > 0`".
- **Effort.** One hour. Highest value per line in this report.

### 5. Quiz prompts with two right answers
- **What.** `present()` in `core/questions.js:165-193` prompts choose-ru and type with `firstSense(w)`
  and rejects distractors only by bare form, so a unit that teaches both «кот» and «кошка» ("cat") shows
  both as options with one marked wrong, and "Write it in Russian: coat" accepts only «пиджак» (which
  is a jacket) or only «пальто», whichever was drawn.
- **Evidence (`a9_quiz.py`).** 95 groups / **202 unit words (19 %)** share a first sense inside their own
  unit; 17 groups share the *entire* gloss (уйти/уехать/уходить "leave, depart"; понимать/понять;
  слышать/услышать; спина/спинка "back"; мышь/мышка; певец/певица; кредит/кредитный; виновный/
  виноватый; сражение/битва; молодёжь/юность). Lesson quizzes use the unit as the pool (`poolFor`), so
  these collide inside a lesson, not only in Practice.
- **Fix (pipeline first).** Give the gloss a distinguishing sense at build time from what the lexicon
  already knows: gender/aspect/POS (кот "cat (tom)", кошка "cat (female)"; понять "understand (pf.)";
  кредитный "credit (adj.)"); a curated override for the rest (пиджак → "jacket"). Then in
  `questions.js`, exclude distractors with the same first sense (one line) and let `type` accept any
  pool word with the same first sense.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 6. Grammar cards: three errors, five units without a card
- `politics` — "the noun goes into the genitive **plural**", example «ма́ло вре́мени» is genitive
  singular (`grammar_notes.json:146-147`).
- `core4` — rule "masculine and feminine nouns usually take -ы or -и", first example «дом → дома́»
  is the stressed -а́ exception the card does not mention (`:30-31`). Use «стол → столы́».
- `food` — "пить … follows neither pattern: я пью, ты пьёшь" — пить is a regular first-conjugation
  (-ешь/-ёшь) verb with a stem change; only есть is irregular (`:66`).
- medicine, business, law, science, religion have no `g` at all (27 of 32 units do), so their first
  lesson opens without the card and `qGrammar` can never ask about them.
- **Fix.** Edit the JSON; add five notes (the instrumental with «с» is already claimed by military, so
  science could take «измерять + instrumental», law «обвинять в + prepositional», etc.).
- **Effort.** An hour.

### 7. Drill data the learner sees raw
- **Aspect drill / entry pill.** `pt` is shipped verbatim; 47 verbs (15 in units: говорить
  "сказать;поговорить", носить "износить;наносить;сносить;относить", читать "прочитать;почитать;
  прочесть", готовить, пить, есть, делать, видеть, бояться, учиться, научиться, плакать, работать,
  выпить, поговорить) show the whole string as one option in `qAspect` and as `pair: сказать;поговорить`
  on the Word screen (`Word.js:99`). Four partners carry OpenRussian's apostrophe stress (§23 trap):
  плыть "поплы'ть", писатель "писа'тельница", выходить "вы'йти"/"выха'живать".
- **Agreement drill.** `qAgreement` asks "___ часы" and marks «но́вый» right: the lexicon knows
  `pl_only` but the payload does not ship it. Eligible offenders in units: часы, родители, каникулы,
  шахматы, выборы, данные, лета (7); деньги, брюки, похороны are saved only by having no gender.
- **Fix (pipeline).** In `build_site.py` split `pt` on ";" into a list and run the apostrophe→acute
  conversion on it (`build_lexicon.py` already has it for headwords); ship `pl`/`sg`/`ind` flags; skip
  `pl_only` nouns in `qAgreement` and use the plural column when a plural noun is chosen.
- **Effort.** Two hours.

### 8. Example sentences are chosen by id, not by readability
- **What.** `build_site.py:164` takes the first four sentences *sorted by item id*. `measure_sentences`
  computes a difficulty and a unit for every sentence but the dictionary never uses them.
- **Evidence (`a2_examples.py`).** Of 4,240 example rows on unit words, 3,681 (87 %) contain a token
  outside the 4,006-lemma index; 2,357 (56 %) have ≥ 2 unknown tokens or ≥ 3 tokens taught in a later
  chapter; chapter 1: 204/430 (47 %). Chapter-1 «вечерний» is illustrated by «До вечерних сумерек звенят
  пилы, стучат топоры и бьют молотки»; «близнец» by «Однояйцевые близнецы наследуют от родителей
  абсолютно идентичный набор генов». Cloze already re-ranks by unknown-token count (`clozeFor`); the
  entry and the flashcard back do not.
- **Fix.** Rank refs by (unit position ≤ this word's unit, unknown tokens, length) using the numbers
  `measure_sentences` already has; keep his own sentences first. Effort: two hours.

### 9. Examples attached through a shared form illustrate a different word
- **Evidence (`a9_quiz.py`).** 236 of 4,239 unit-word examples are attached only through a form key the
  lemma shares and does not own in the index (66 words). Beyond the stubs of finding 2: лето ← «Моему
  старшему брату 35 лет», мочь ← «Он страдает недержанием **мочи**» (urine), мыть ← «Мой муж носит
  усы», столовый ← «Мужчина в столовой ест борщ», лук ← «Отправляемся дальше в Великие Луки»,
  сыр ← forms of сырой, полка ← полк, дорога ← дорогой, техник ← техника, вина ← вино, горе ← гора,
  спор ← спора, часы ← час, супруга ← супруг.
- **Fix.** In `build_dictionary`, when the only tokens tying a sentence to the lemma are shared keys,
  require the resolver (finding 2) to pick this lemma. Effort: with finding 2 done, an hour.

### 10. The same sentence twice
- 327 of 1,070 unit words show a sentence twice — the Core 5000 stressed twin of an Ultimate Guide
  sentence («Нарежьте ветчину и лук» / «Наре́жьте ветчину́ и лук») — so "4 examples" is often 2; 13 words
  have one sentence shown twice as their whole set. The pool holds 23,930 rows, 19,931 distinct by
  `fold`: 3,999 twins. **Fix:** dedupe `sentence_ref` by `fold(ru)`, keep the stressed one (it carries
  the better recording too). Effort: an hour.

### 11. Video library: chips are honest; unit episodes are not
- Moment integrity is clean: 18,054 moments (17,446 library + 608 unit), **0** where the spoken form is
  not a lexicon form of the listed lemma, **0** where the snippet lacks the form; 30 sampled moments
  all correct; 1,465 (8 %) came through the ambiguity resolver and the top credits (тут, дело, минут,
  скажем, чай, мире, жила, тепло) are right.
- But the chip's *entry* is finding 2: 149 «тут» moments open "mulberry fruit"; «дело» opens деть in
  search. And a unit's own episode says few of its words: science **0/21**, core7 4/28, religion 4/22,
  city 5/40 (an `OVERRIDES` pick), military 5/39, tech 5/40 (override), core4 6/28. `build_videos.py:291-
  299` hands every spine unit the beginner series regardless of coverage.
- **Fix.** Require `hit ≥ 5` for a unit episode (as the ranked path already does), otherwise ship no
  `v` for the unit rather than a video that says none of its words; drop or re-pick the two overrides.
  Effort: an hour. Minor: `heardIn` (data.js:52) sorts by count only, the comment promises the unit's
  episode first.

### 16. Placement and section tests test the right thing and inherit the wrong data
- `placementQuestions` samples ~7 words per spine unit (spine only — consistent with side quests being
  optional); `sectionQuestions` samples per lesson of the unit. Both use the unit as the distractor pool.
- Because 26 of 224 spine words are stubs or wrong (finding 2), an expected **~6 of the 50 placement
  questions** land on one: "What does this mean? — тут" has "mulberry fruit" as the right answer and a
  learner who knows Russian is marked wrong; "Write it in Russian: genitive of I" is a real prompt.
  `TEST_OUT` is 0.8 per stage of ~6 questions, so one such question decides a stage.
