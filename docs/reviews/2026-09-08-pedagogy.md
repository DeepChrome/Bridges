# Bridges — pedagogy review (2026-09-08)

Read-only review of the native app as a learner would meet it. Numbers come from
`native/assets/data.json` (4,006 lemmas, 32 units, 169 lessons, 1,070 taught words,
1,912 pooled sentences), from `node tools/simulate.mjs --seed 3` run this session,
and from the sources cited. Nothing in the repository was changed; the simulator's
two output files were removed afterwards (see the note at the end).

## Verdict

A learner who opens this every day will learn to *recognise* about a thousand
Russian words, hear them in real recordings, and look any word up beautifully. He
will not learn to *speak* Russian from it in its current shape, because the first
chapter (22 lessons, roughly eleven days) teaches 108 words of which one is a verb,
the grammar is shown once on a card and never asked, 85% of quiz questions are
multiple choice, and review only happens if he leaves the path and ticks boxes.
What most limits it is not the mechanics — the runner, scheduler, pools and word
links are genuinely good — but the curriculum cut: the spine is a raw frequency band
of his decks, the branches are 40-noun banks, and 210 of the 463 commonest words
(да, или, если, два, надо, ждать, пойти) are taught nowhere.

## Findings, ranked by impact on learning

### 1. Chapter 1 contains one verb; verbs arrive in chapter 7

- **Evidence.** Verb census by chapter (script over the payload): ch1 **1** verb of
  108 words (быть); ch2 9; ch3 5; ch4 13; ch5 8; ch6 4; ch7 34; ch8 10. Chapter 1's
  spine `core1` is 8 pronouns, 8 prepositions/particles, 6 determiners
  (этот, весь, тот, свой, мой, такой), 3 nouns, 1 numeral, 1 verb. Its branches:
  family 39 nouns + 1 adjective, time 31 nouns + 9 adjectives. At `LESSON_RAMP[0]=5`
  (`core/questions.js:50`) that is 22 lessons. читать is taught in `core8`,
  говорить in `core3`, идти in `core5`, ходить never, пойти never.
- **Why it matters.** Every sequence in `docs/grammar-sequence.md` puts present-tense
  verbs in the first two or three lessons, because a learner with pronouns and nouns
  and no verb cannot make a sentence, and cannot do anything with «мой брат» for
  eleven days. This is the single largest reason the app would feel like flashcards
  despite the doctrine (§2, §26). The grammar cards themselves need verbs
  (finding 4).
- **Change.** In `tools/build_topics.py`, give each spine unit a verb quota: the
  unit is the next `SPINE_UNIT − V` words by frequency plus the `V` (≈6) commonest
  verbs not yet placed, drawn from the same frequency ranking. Cap early branches
  (finding 9). Re-run `audit_branches.py` and the simulator and diff.
- **Effort.** 1 day.

### 2. 210 of the 463 most frequent words are never taught

- **Evidence.** Distinct bare forms with `fr ≤ 500`: 463; not in any unit: **210**,
  including да, или, если, надо, нужно, два, пять, никогда, ждать, играть, пойти,
  увидеть, иметь, больше, теперь, тоже, ли, даже, часто. Cause: the spine is
  `SPINE_UNIT * 8 = 224` words (`build_topics.py:450`) and branches admit only nouns,
  verbs and adjectives whose gloss matches a topic rule (`BRANCHABLE`, `RULES`), so
  an adverb, conjunction or numeral outside the top 224 falls out of the curriculum
  entirely. The pools then treat these as "free" (`COVERAGE_FREE_RANK = 500`,
  `build_site.py:224`): in the chapter-2 listening pool **17% of tokens are untaught
  and 30 of 49 sentences carry an untaught word** («Он не пошёл, и я тоже», «Я ждал и
  ждал», «Да, ну и что?»); still 10% at chapter 5.
- **Why it matters.** «да» is not a word one can meet "on every screen" if no lesson
  ever shows it. Dictation (Hear) in chapter 2 asks the learner to type words he has
  never seen, and grades them Again; the trouble bank fills with them.
- **Change.** Extend the spine's reach: either raise the spine to ~12 units of 28
  (the frequency band is the right lane for these words), or add a closed-class lane
  per chapter ("Small words", 10–15 items: conjunctions, adverbs, numerals) built
  from frequency rank with no topic rule. Then lower `COVERAGE_FREE_RANK` toward what
  the spine actually teaches by that point.
- **Effort.** Half a day for the data; a day with the audit.

### 3. Grammar is shown once, never asked

- **Evidence.** A unit's card appears once, at `index === 0` of the vocabulary flow
  (`core/questions.js:360`, `Flows.js:66-84`). No quiz kind asks about grammar: the
  lesson quiz draws from `candidates()` (choose-en, listen, choose-ru, cloze, type —
  `questions.js:140-148`). The only grammar practice is in Practice: `qGrammar` asks
  "which rule does this show" (rule-name recognition, `questions.js:658-670`), and
  `qCases`/`qAgreement`/`qConjugation` pick a random cell of the full table
  (`questions.js:553-576`) — a chapter-4 learner whose card says "Making things
  plural" can be asked the instrumental plural. `core/errortags.js` links tags to
  units, but `tagCounts` fill only from the Worker's Say feedback (chapter 3+,
  online).
- **Why it matters.** Case endings are the skill; exposure to a two-sentence card
  does not build it, and nothing in the path checks whether it did.
- **Change.** One new question kind, `form`: from the unit's note and the
  paradigm tables the payload already carries, "put ___ in the form this sentence
  needs" — choose (early) or type (later) the prepositional of a chapter-5 noun,
  the plural of a chapter-4 noun, the past of a chapter-7 verb. One per quiz from
  the unit's own card; gate `qCases` by the cases introduced so far
  (`unitsUpTo`). Generator case, `VIEWS` entry (`Run.js:240`), `registry.test.js`.
- **Effort.** 2–3 days.

### 4. The grammar cards use words the learner has not met

- **Evidence.** `data/curated/grammar_notes.json`: `core2` conjugates читать (taught
  `core8`) and говорить (`core3`); `core6` «Я читаю книгу» and `core7` «Он читал»
  (читать, chapter 8); `core8` прочитать (never taught); `travel` contrasts идти
  (`core5`, the next chapter) with ходить (never taught); `core1` «Он дома» (дома
  untaught). `core3`'s «новая книга» is fine (chapter 2 and 3).
- **Why it matters.** A card whose example needs a word two chapters ahead is read
  as noise; and it is the direct symptom of finding 1 (the textbook verbs are not on
  the spine).
- **Change.** A build-time check in `build_site.py`: every token of a note's examples
  must resolve to a word taught by that unit or earlier on the route; print the
  offenders. Then either rewrite the six cards from the chapter's own words or (with
  finding 1) pull читать, говорить, идти, ходить, жить, работать onto the spine where
  the cards need them.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 5. "Production" in the quiz is mostly multiple choice, and cloze asks for the wrong form

- **Evidence.** `quizSteps` adds two "production" steps chosen as
  `candidates(i).filter(type || cloze)[0]` (`questions.js:446-449`); the list order is
  cloze then type, and ~97% of words have an example, so the slot is almost always
  cloze. Simulator (seed 3, quick): cloze 173 of 486 quiz questions (36%), type 71
  (15%); the teaching phase retrieves with `candidates(r)[0]` = choose-en only
  (`questions.js:365`). Cloze options are bare lemmas (`label: L[i].b`,
  `questions.js:186`) while the gap held an inflected token: «Я не хочу _____» is
  answered «пиво», the sentence needed «пива», and the restored sentence is never
  shown.
- **Why it matters.** Recognition is not recall; the doctrine asks whether
  "retrieval practice is real". Fifteen percent typed, zero spoken until chapter 3,
  and the one contextual exercise teaches the learner to ignore endings.
- **Change.** Production slots prefer `type` (and `say`/`hear` once available);
  cloze options are surface forms — the removed token plus three distractors that
  are other cells of the same paradigm when the table has them — and the verdict
  shows the sentence whole with the form named by `describeForm` (`core/forms.js`).
- **Effort.** 1 day.

### 6. A new word becomes a "leech" inside one session

- **Evidence.** `core/fsrs.js:65,73` counts every Again as a lapse and raises
  difficulty; `isTrouble` is lapses ≥ 4 or d ≥ 8.5 with reps ≥ 3. Measured with the
  real functions: Again, Again, Good on the same day → `trouble = true` after three
  reviews. The runner grades retries ("the retake is still a review",
  `Run.js:339-350`), quiz retakes regenerate 8+ questions on the same five words, and
  Study re-queues an Again card in the same run (`Study.js:292`). Simulator, 40
  lessons: struggling 73 leeches of 212 words (34%), steady 23, quick 14 (7%).
- **Why it matters.** The trouble bank is meant to hold persistent difficulty (§26).
  Instead it lists yesterday's new words; Study then weights them ×1000
  (`Study.js:35`) over genuinely overdue material, and the learner is told he has
  seventy problems after a month.
- **Change.** Anki's rule: a lapse is an Again on a card that had graduated
  (`card.last < now`, interval ≥ 1 day); same-day repeats are learning steps that
  neither add a lapse nor move difficulty. Pin it in `core.test.mjs`; rerun the
  simulator and expect leeches to fall to the genuinely hard words.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 7. Review never enters the path

- **Evidence.** `nextLesson` (`data.js:246`) ignores what is due; `Learn.js` has no
  mention of due or review (grep); Study opens on `sets: []` ("Nothing selected",
  `store.js:25`, `Study.js:306`); the only due count is a statistic on the You
  screen (`You.js:201,230`). When nothing is due Study shows the whole set anyway
  (`buildQueue`: `if (!q.length) q = pool.slice()`, `Study.js:68`). Simulator: 16–31
  reviews a day at 212 words; at 1,070 words that is 80–150 a day for someone who
  never presses Study.
- **Why it matters.** FSRS is only as good as the reviews that reach it; a learner
  who does what the path tells him — "Continue" — accumulates debt invisibly, and
  the app cannot "know what I am struggling with".
- **Change.** (a) A "Review · N due" row on Learn above Continue, queue = all due in
  `seen`, Study's own screen. (b) The quiz's top-up slots (`questions.js:450-455`)
  take due words from `seen` before earlier-lesson words. (c) Study's nothing-due
  state says so and offers "study ahead" explicitly rather than silently cramming.
- **Effort.** 1–2 days.

### 8. Chapter 8 is a 44-lesson dump and the hard grammar lands last

- **Evidence.** Lessons per chapter: 22, 19, 15, 16, 19, 19, 15, **44** (chapter 8:
  9 units, 290 words — military, tech, sport, art, politics, science, law, religion).
  Aspect is the last spine card; future, imperative, reflexives, comparatives and
  dative of age have no card at all (`docs/grammar-sequence.md:39-41`, which says so).
  `STAGE_PLAN` puts eight branches on the eighth spine (`build_topics.py:549`).
- **Why it matters.** A learner reaches "Two aspects of every verb" with 10 verbs in
  the chapter and then faces 40 lessons of nouns; the course ends as a word bank.
- **Change.** Two more spine units (frequency band 225–280, which finding 2 needs
  anyway) carrying future / imperative / dative cards; redistribute the late branches
  across chapters 6–10 by the grammar they can use.
- **Effort.** 1 day.

### 9. Branch units are 40-noun word banks

- **Evidence.** Part-of-speech per branch: family 39/40 nouns; art 38/40; military
  36/39; tech 36/40; home 35/40; food 35/40; nature 31 nouns 8 adj 1 verb. Only
  emotion (13 verbs) and speech (17) are mixed. `BRANCH_MAX = 40`
  (`build_topics.py:30`); rules are gloss-matched, and verbs seldom match a topic
  noun list.
- **Why it matters.** Doctrine §2 lists "a series of word banks" as what the app
  must never feel like, and §26 says not to turn every objective into a flashcard.
  Eight lessons of family nouns with no verb is exactly that.
- **Change.** `BRANCH_MAX` by chapter (20 in chapters 1–3), and a per-branch verb
  list in `RULES` (family: любить, жить, звать; food: есть, пить, готовить, заказать;
  city: идти, ходить, находиться…) so each branch teaches 4–6 verbs it can use with
  its nouns. Audit with `audit_branches.py`.
- **Effort.** 1 day.

### 10. Sentence "difficulty" is measured against the dictionary, not the learner

- **Evidence.** `build_site.py:252`: `studied = tokens in index`, where the index is
  all 4,006 lemmas; every chapter-2 listening row has difficulty 0.00. Gating is done
  by the coverage rule alone, which admits any top-500 word (finding 2).
- **Why it matters.** The field rides in the payload and the roadmap (P2.1) as if it
  ordered prompts by hardness; it does not, and nothing orders them. A Hear prompt
  early in chapter 2 is as likely to be «Тогда мне было всего четыре года» as «Что ты
  хочешь?».
- **Change.** Compute difficulty per unit position (share of tokens taught by
  `unitsUpTo`), ship it, and have `pickPrompt` (`core/speech.js:30`) prefer the
  easiest available rather than uniform random.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 11. Word cards and flashcards show unsorted examples; only cloze picks the readable one

- **Evidence.** `Flows.js:106` shows `w.x.slice(0, 2)`; `Study.js:350` `slice(0, 3)`;
  `Word.js` all. Lesson 1's card for «в» opens on «Это явление продолжает
  оставаться в фокусе внимания экспертов» and «Он лежал в гробу» (He was lying in a
  coffin); «я» on «Я сел на коня». `clozeFor` (`questions.js:107-124`) already sorts
  by unknown tokens then length — for cloze only.
- **Why it matters.** The first Russian a beginner reads on his first card should be
  three words long; these are the sentences the doctrine's "vocabulary lives in
  context" rule is meant to deliver.
- **Change.** Sort `x` once at hydration (`core/entry.js`) by (all tokens in index,
  tokens taught up to the word's own unit, length) — the same key `clozeFor` uses —
  so every consumer gets the readable sentence first.
- **Effort.** 2–3 hours.

### 12. No sentence-level listening or speaking for the first 22 lessons

- **Evidence.** `SPEECH_MIX` (`questions.js:21-25`): hear and scene from stage 1,
  say from stage 2. The comment's reason ("a learner who has met forty words") is out
  of date — chapter 1 is 108 words and 22 lessons. The pools do exist for it: core1
  adds 32 speak and 6 listen rows (`LISTEN_TOKENS = (4, 15)` excludes the short ones).
- **Why it matters.** Eleven days of reading before the ear is used on a sentence is
  the opposite of Pimsleur/Assimil, and the recordings are the product's best asset.
- **Change.** Hear from chapter 1 lesson 3 onward drawing from the speak pool when
  the listen pool is under `POOL_MIN_PER_UNIT`; `LISTEN_TOKENS` minimum 3 for chapter
  1. Say stays at chapter 3 (the STT gate was measured on 2–4-word sentences, so
  chapter 2 is defensible too).
- **Effort.** 2 hours plus a rebuild.

### 13. Scene and Say grade every word of a sentence from one outcome

- **Evidence.** Scene: a right meaning choice grades all lemmas of the sentence Good,
  a wrong one grades them all Again (`Scene.js:80-84`); a perfect first Say/Hear
  grades every lemma Easy, «не» and «и» included (`core/speech.js:72`).
- **Why it matters.** A learner who recognises one keyword and guesses the English
  banks six Good reviews; one wrong guess is six lapses toward finding 6. The
  scheduler's picture of what is known drifts from what was tested.
- **Change.** Scene grades the "which word did you hear" lemma and only content
  words (rank > `SCENE_SKIP_TOP`) of a sentence, Good at most; a wrong meaning
  grades nothing (comprehension, not per-word recall). Sentence activities cap at
  Good; Easy stays for the flashcard buttons where the learner says so.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 14. The spine carries frequency artefacts of the decks

- **Evidence.** `core2`: «лёт» (the shared form «лет», years); `core3`: «быль» (from
  «был»), «евро», «Россия», «компания»; `core2`/`core3` list «меня», «мне», «его»,
  «её», «всё», «все» as headwords of their own while «я» is in `core1` — "I" is taught
  as four words, «читать» once, in chapter 8. `STUBS` names only двух and житься
  (`build_topics.py:430`).
- **Why it matters.** Chapter 1–3 is where trust is won; a lesson on «быль» or a
  quiz distractor «лёт» reads as a bug.
- **Change.** A rule rather than a list: a candidate bare form that is a paradigm
  cell of another lemma already on the spine is a stub unless whitelisted
  (`function_words.json` has the pronoun paradigms); and pronoun case forms teach
  as forms of «я» (a `form` question, finding 3), not as words.
- **Effort.** 2–3 hours plus the audit.

### 15. Trouble words return only as flashcards

- **Evidence.** `troubleWords` feeds the Study set `__trouble__` and the You list;
  `quizSteps` never prefers them; `drillPool` (`data.js:281`) treats every seen word
  alike; `listeningDrill` passes `want = null` (`questions.js:292`).
- **Why it matters.** §26: difficult material should return "through different
  contexts and activity types"; a leech drilled as the same flashcard stays a leech.
- **Change.** Quiz top-up and drill pools weight trouble first; the Listening drill
  and `speechPrompt` pass the trouble lemmas as `want`, so a hard word comes back
  inside a sentence.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 16. Hear grades a wrong ending as a whole-word lapse and says nothing about it

- **Evidence.** `compare` aligns through `fold` at word level; «книга» for «книгу» is
  `sub` → Again for книга (`speech.js:72`). The `type` kind has one-letter relief
  (`Run.js:76-78`); Hear has none, and the Alignment shows red without naming the
  form.
- **Why it matters.** With 15% untaught tokens in the pool (finding 2), the beginner's
  commonest error in dictation is the ending; punishing it as a lost word and not
  saying which case it was teaches nothing about cases.
- **Change.** In `gradeAlignment`, a `sub` with `charDistance ≤ 2` and the same
  lemma is "near": Hard, half credit, and the verdict names the expected form via
  `describeForm`.
- **Effort.** Half a day.

### 17. Talk arrives late and is pitched high

- **Evidence.** `TALK_UNLOCK_STAGE = 4` (`Talk.js:41`) — after chapter 5's spine,
  ~90 lessons in — while the meet/café/flat scenarios are chapter 1–3 vocabulary
  (`core/scenarios.js`). Level defaults to intermediate from chapter 3 (§30h′) when
  the learner knows ~330 words and 15 verbs. `studiedFor` says "strongest first"
  but `drillPool` iterates `Object.keys(st.seen)` — insertion order (`data.js:292`).
- **Why it matters.** The one activity that demands free production is withheld
  until the learner has done 90 multiple-choice lessons.
- **Change.** Unlock Talk with chapter 2's spine (scenarios already gate by unit);
  beginner through chapter 4; sort `studiedFor` by stability so the prompt really
  gets the strongest 300.
- **Effort.** 2 hours.

### 18. The simulator's evidence, and what it does not cover

- **Run this session** (`--seed 3`, 40 lessons, chapters 1–2 only): quick 40/40
  passed (2 on relief), 14 leeches; steady 40/40 (1), 23 leeches; struggling 38/40
  (12 on relief), chapter-2 accuracy 0.61, first-try passes 3 of 18, 73 leeches.
  Reviews 16–31 a day, again rate ≤ 0.07, no backlog. Quiz mix: cloze 35%, type 15%,
  sentence steps 11% (chapter 2 only). Structural problems 0.
- **What it says.** The pass mark works and relief is doing real work for the weak
  learner (12 of 38 passes). Leech counts are the artefact of finding 6, not of the
  words. The mix confirms finding 5.
- **What it cannot say.** It stops at chapter 2 of 8 (40 of 169 lessons); it does
  not recycle questions (`Run.js` does), so the real per-word grading load is
  higher; its Study session is a meaning question, not the four-button flashcard;
  nobody has simulated chapters 3–8, where Say, the cases and aspect drills and the
  44-lesson chapter 8 live. Run it at `--lessons 169` before any curriculum change
  is judged.
- **Effort.** 2 hours to extend (recycling, full route, per-chapter leech and review
  curves).

## What is genuinely strong — do not break these

- **Coverage-gated pools** (`measure_sentences`, `unitsUpTo`): a quiz never asks a
  sentence from further along the route, and asks nothing rather than something too
  hard. Fix the *free rank*, keep the rule.
- **Two-press word links with the form named** (`words.js`, `core/forms.js`): the
  sheet answers "which word, which form" without leaving the sentence. This is the
  product's differentiator and it is done right.
- **Real audio, honestly labelled**, 13,368 recordings, device voice never passed
  off as authentic; the audio-never-cut runner rule; unlimited replays in Hear.
- **The runner's grading contract**: partial credit, first-attempt scoring, recycling
  for practice not marks, hint costs the grade, skip leaves the total alone. Say's
  local verdict never waits for the network.
- **Scene's "read the questions first"** and its refusal to build a two-option scene.
- **Readable cloze selection** (`clozeFor`) — extend it, do not replace it.
- **Relief rule and test-out routes**: a stuck learner moves; a returning one skips.
- **State keyed on the Russian string; migrations in core** — history survives
  rebuilds and platform moves.
- **The instruments**: a seeded simulator with structural checks and an emulator
  walkthrough. Every finding above can be re-measured after its fix.

## Open questions only the owner can answer

1. **Is deck frequency sacred?** Findings 1, 2, 8, 9 and 14 all re-cut the spine by
   pedagogy (verb quota, closed-class lane, more spine units). That departs from
   "the spine is a frequency band of *my* decks". Which matters more: fidelity to the
   corpus order, or being able to say a sentence in week one?
2. **Should review be part of Continue?** Duolingo-style (due cards first, then the
   lesson) or a separate tab with a visible count? Finding 7 works either way; the
   choice changes the Learn screen.
3. **What is a leech to you?** Anki's definition (lapses on graduated cards) or the
   current one (any Again, including a first-day miss)? Finding 6 assumes Anki's.
4. **What is the target?** TORFL A1 by chapter 5? A2 by the end? The sequence cannot
   be judged complete without one; today the future tense, imperative and
   reflexives have no home.
5. **Shorter branches?** 20-word side quests mean more units and a busier map. Is
   the map's shape or the lesson's shape the thing to protect?
6. **Talk from chapter 2 at beginner level** costs Worker calls earlier; is that
   within the budget you want?
7. **Developer mode is on for you.** Everything is unlocked, so you have never seen
   the path as a learner sees it — the fork, the locks, the relief message. Would a
   second profile with dev off be worth keeping for that reason?
