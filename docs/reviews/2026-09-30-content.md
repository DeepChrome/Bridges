# Content review, chapter by chapter — 2026-09-30

*Written for: the owner, deciding what to buy next; and whoever reviews a
chapter after this.*

The owner's rule for the review: **"When in doubt, stick to the most
useful/common words. Build towards the videos. The exposure to those
words/grammar/sentences will help the learner understand the general video.
They don't need to know every single word."**

## What changed, and how it is held

- **A side quest teaches its topic.** Built from its video alone, a quest took
  whatever the episode happened to say: Sport taught «автомат» (machine gun),
  «акция» (share) and «трагический»; Animals «банкомат» (ATM); Faith
  «скидка» (discount). Now (`tools/build_topics.py`):
  - an off-topic video word joins a quest only if it is common — among the
    thousand words the most videos say (`OFFTOPIC_RANK`);
  - and at most a quarter of a quest is such words (`OFFTOPIC_SHARE`); the
    rest are the topic's own words. Common words not taken fall to the
    spine, which teaches the commonest words anyway.
- **A reader's keeps and drops** live in `data/curated/unit_words.json`, one
  entry per unit, applied on every build. This file is the review's output
  and is meant to be edited: a word a unit should teach goes under `keep`,
  a word it should not under `drop`.
- **Home's goal video** is now the apartment tour; the dacha vlog it had
  taught «лето» and «салат» and left the rooms of a home to other units
  (`data/curated/skeleton.json`).
- **«уж»** is the particle ("really"), not the grass snake
  (`lemma_overrides.json`); **«мясо»** is glossed "meat", **«получиться»**
  "to turn out" (`gloss_overrides.json`).
- 356 words changed unit; 1,031 are taught. How much of each goal video a
  learner can follow moved by 0–3 points in most units — the videos' bulk is
  common words the spine teaches — and fell in Family and Food (to about
  60 %), which is the trade the owner chose.

### 2. The family as the vocabulary — about 12 pictures, about $3

Where a word *is* a character, draw the character: мама → Nezha, папа →
Yarik, сын → Teddy, подруга → Belka, бабушка/дедушка → Tortila and an old
bear, семья → all of them, обезьяна → Monka, белка → Belka, волк → Yarik,
собака → Teddy. Cheap, instantly memorable, and the brand on every card.

### 3. "Teddy acts it out" — verbs and describing words, medium quality

Photographs fail exactly where a beginner needs a picture most: verbs and
adjectives (a Wikipedia photo of "to want" does not exist — §30h′ blanked
most of them). About **180** of the taught words are verbs and adjectives
that can be acted: пить, спать, бегать, плакать, смеяться, горячий,
холодный, голодный, грустный, весёлый, большой, маленький… Teddy (or
whoever fits) doing it, one picture each. At medium quality roughly
**$10–15**; at high quality about $38. Suggest one chapter first to judge
the look.

### 4. Not worth buying

Function words («и», «что», «уже») and abstractions («смысл», «принцип»):
no picture beats a clear word and its sound.
