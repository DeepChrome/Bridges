# The listening scenario, on the emulator (CLAUDE.md 30l).
#
#   .\native\tools\walk.ps1 -Flow native\flows\listening.txt -Device emulator
#
# Starts from the launcher, so force-stop the app first if it is already open.
# What to look at in the shots: the five questions are all readable before
# anything plays; the transport is three controls and a bar; the cast names are
# on screen (they are what half the questions are about); and after Check, the
# conversation is there with a speaker over every line.

# Home first: force-stopping the app leaves its dead task on screen, not the
# launcher, so the icon is not there to tap.
key:3
wait:2
tap:Bridges
wait:12
expect:Learn
shot:00-path

tapn:Practice:0
wait:3
expect:A conversation at the level you are on
shot:01-practice

# By its subtitle: "Listening" alone also matches the section label above it.
tap:A conversation at the level you are on
wait:4
expect:LISTEN, THEN ANSWER
expect:Play the conversation
expect:Back five seconds
texts
shot:02-scenario

# Play it. The lines are spoken one at a time by the device voices; the bar and
# the speaker chips move while it runs.
tap:Play the conversation
wait:6
shot:03-playing

# Back five seconds, then from the start: both have to work mid-playback.
tap:Back five seconds
wait:3
shot:04-back

tap:Play from the start
wait:3
shot:05-restart
